# Product Vision

## One sentence
NEON FUSE is a fast browser multiplayer arena game where players trap, out-position and out-think each other with timed energy cores in destructible retro-futuristic arenas.

## Audience
- Players who want instant, social, replayable rounds.
- Retro/arcade players who value readable rules and high mastery.
- Creators who may later design maps, cosmetic items and themes.

## Core pillars
1. **Readable in 30 seconds** — movement + place core.
2. **Deep through interaction** — timing, prediction, positioning, chain reactions, map control.
3. **Social first** — multiplayer is not an add-on.
4. **Fair competition** — paid content cannot confer combat advantage.
5. **Strong identity** — CRT / late-90s cyber / terminal / arcade aesthetic, not a clone of any historical franchise.
6. **Creator extensibility** — content interfaces should later support maps and cosmetics.

## Initial match loop
Lobby → ready → spawn → destroy blocks → acquire temporary match power-ups → eliminate opponents → sudden death → winner → rematch.

## Non-goals for MVP
- Blockchain gameplay dependencies.
- Pay-to-win upgrades.
- Large persistent open worlds.
- 32-player launch target.
- Physics-heavy simulation.
- User-generated executable code.
