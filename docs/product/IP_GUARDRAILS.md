# IP Guardrails

NEON FUSE may be inspired by broad historical game mechanics, but it must remain an original product.

Do not copy from existing franchises:
- names or trademarks
- characters or silhouettes distinctive to a franchise
- sprites, textures, maps or UI layouts
- music or sound effects
- story/lore
- distinctive item names/art
- level layouts
- marketing language

Allowed design inspiration is limited to abstract mechanics and genre conventions such as grid movement, timed area attacks, destructible blocks, pickups, last-player-standing and team modes.

Every shipped visual/audio/content asset must be original, commissioned, generated under acceptable terms, or used under a verified compatible license.
